The process

How we deliver the full basket.

Four steps, eight hours from order to doorstep. Every Premio order flows through the same temperature-controlled, slot-scheduled system — whether it's a carton of water or the entire weekly shop.

01

Browse & build your basket

Shop across every department on web or WhatsApp. Add anything from a carton of water to a ribeye steak. Minimum order ₦5,000.

02

Choose your 30-minute slot

A seven-day calendar with live capacity. Morning slots from 6:30am. Lock it by 11pm the night before.

03

Picked & packed after midnight

Your order is picked overnight in our cold warehouse. Chilled, ambient, and frozen go into separate insulated crates — tri-temperature from shelf to van.

04

Tracked to your door

Follow your van live. Your driver calls 15 minutes before arrival and delivers inside your 30-minute window, cold chain unbroken.
