About Premio

Everyday luxury. Pure curated quality.

Premio exists for one reason: the households of Lagos Island deserve a grocer built to their standard. Not a marketplace of everything, but a cold warehouse of the right things — every product chosen, every temperature held, every delivery on time.

Curation

199 products, each one chosen

We don't stock aisles — we edit them. Every line in our eighteen departments is there because it's the best answer to what a household actually uses, from Peruvian avocados to French brie to the washing powder that works.

Cold chain

2°C from warehouse to door

Our own dark store, UK-standard refrigerated vans, tri-temperature crates. The cold chain is not a marketing line — it is the operating system of the business.

Precision

The 30-minute window

Groceries that arrive "sometime today" are not a service. Premio delivers in a slot you choose, with a driver who calls fifteen minutes out — and a credit on us if we miss it.

Territory

Lagos Island first, properly

Banana Island, Victoria Island, Ikoyi and Lekki Phase 1 — served completely before we expand. Gate access coordinated in advance, handover at concierge or door.

The standard

Built like the grocers we admire.

Premio is modelled on the world's best grocery operations — the slot discipline of the great British online grocers, the curation of a fine food hall, the cold-chain rigour of a wholesaler — assembled for Lagos, by people who shop here. We are a Nigerian company with a global standard, registered in Lagos and built for the long term.
